# FX Vision

**Program:** Forge Experience (FX)  
**Version:** FX-S0  
**Status:** APPROVED

## One ecosystem

Users should instantly understand any Forge product without retraining.

- Moving from **Forge RMS** to **Forge Academy** should feel identical.
- Moving from **Forge Academy** to **Forge Industrial Safety** should feel identical.
- Only **business logic** should change.
- Never **user experience**.

## Product architecture

```text
Forge Experience
│
├── Forge RMS
├── Forge Academy
├── Forge Industrial Safety
└── Future Products
```

Each product extends FX.

- No product replaces FX.
- No product duplicates FX.

## Emotional and operational target

Forge Experience must feel like:

- Mission Control
- Emergency Operations Center
- Modern public safety platform
- Industrial operations center

It must **not** feel like:

- Spreadsheet software
- Legacy government software
- Generic CRM
- Form builder
- Database viewer

## North-star outcomes

| Outcome              | Meaning                                                                       |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Instant literacy     | A user trained on one Forge product can operate another without UX retraining |
| Role clarity         | Users work from responsibilities, not database collections                    |
| Record gravity       | Work orbits operational records, not forms-as-apps                            |
| Attention discipline | Screens answer what is happening, what needs attention, and what to do next   |
| Inclusive operation  | WCAG 2.2 AA is a baseline, not an afterthought                                |
| Device parity        | Desktop, tablet, mobile, and large dashboard displays are first-class         |

## Permanent role

Forge Experience becomes the permanent UX foundation for the Forge ecosystem.  
Product innovation happens as **extensions**, not replacements.
