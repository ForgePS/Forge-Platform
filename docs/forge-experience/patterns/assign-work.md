# Pattern — Assign Work

**Last Updated:** 2026-07-30 · **Author:** FX Program

## Purpose
Assign or reassign work items/records to people or roles.

## Scope
My Work quick reassign; record Assign action; bulk assign.

## Goals
Person Lookup; permission checks; timeline Assigned event.

## Examples
Assign inspection to company officer.

## Anti-patterns
Free-text assignee without lookup.

## Dependencies
My Work · Forms lookups · Workflow Assigned.
