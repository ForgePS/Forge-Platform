### Rules

1. High/Critical residual risk requires executive sponsor signature (name, date).
2. Default maximum acceptance period: **12 months**.
3. Acceptances must not contradict the claims boundary (no “accepting” false SOC 2 certification claims).
4. Compensating controls, if any, must be listed in `../controls/compensating-controls.md`.
